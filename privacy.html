1. Introduction

Niva Health AI ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following information:

  • Account information: Username, display name, and hashed password
  • Health data: Food diary entries, exercise records, BMI calculations, and daily weight logs that you choose to save
  • Images and videos: Food photos and exercise videos you upload for AI analysis (processed in real-time, not stored on our servers)
  • Device information: Basic device data for app functionality

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide AI-powered food and exercise analysis
  • To store your health diary and progress data
  • To manage your account and authentication
  • To improve app features and performance

4. Data Storage

Your data is securely stored using Google Firebase Firestore, a cloud database service provided by Google LLC. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Images and videos are sent directly to Google's Gemini AI API for analysis and are not permanently stored by us.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase: User authentication and data storage
  • Google Gemini AI API: Food and exercise image analysis

These services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

5.1 AI Data Processing (Google Gemini)

Some features of the App — food photo analysis and exercise video analysis — are powered by Google's Gemini AI API, a third-party generative AI service. When you use these features:

  • What is sent: The food image, exercise video, and/or text description you provide, together with an instruction prompt, is transmitted to Google's Gemini API over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection so that it can generate the analysis.
  • Limited use: This data is used solely to generate the analysis you requested and return it to you. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to this feature.
  • Not used to train AI models: We use the paid Gemini API. Under Google's API terms, content submitted through the paid Gemini API is not used to train Google's AI models.
  • No permanent storage: Images and videos are processed in real time and are not stored on our servers after the analysis is returned to you.
  • Your consent: By choosing to upload a photo or video, or to run an analysis, you consent to this processing. If you prefer not to have your data sent to the AI service, you can simply avoid using the analysis features.

Google's handling of the data it receives is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties, except:

  • With your assigned Dealer (who manages your account), who can view your health diary entries
  • With App administrators for account management purposes
  • When required by law

7. Your Rights

  • Access your personal data at any time through the App
  • Request deletion of your account and data by contacting us
  • Update your profile information within the App

8. Children's Privacy

Niva Health AI is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes through the App.

11. Account Deletion

You have the right to delete your account and all associated data at any time.

How to delete your account:

  • In-app: Log in to Niva Health AI → tap the 🗑 icon in the top bar → enter your password → confirm deletion. All your data will be permanently deleted immediately.
  • By email: Send a deletion request to seanchua711@gmail.com with your username and we will delete your account within 7 days.

Data deleted includes: account information, health diary entries, BMI records, weight logs, and all personal health data stored in our database.
