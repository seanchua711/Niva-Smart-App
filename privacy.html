1. Introduction

Niva Health AI ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following information:

  • Account information: Username, display name, and hashed password
  • Health data: Food diary entries, exercise records, BMI calculations, and daily weight logs that you choose to save
  • Images and videos: Food photos and exercise videos you upload for AI analysis (processed in real-time, not stored on our servers)
  • Device information: Basic device data for app functionality

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide AI-powered food and exercise analysis
  • To store your health diary and progress data
  • To manage your account and authentication
  • To improve app features and performance

4. Data Storage

Your data is securely stored using Google Firebase Firestore, a cloud database service provided by Google LLC. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Images and videos are sent directly to Google's Gemini AI API for analysis and are not permanently stored by us.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase: User authentication and data storage
  • Google Gemini AI API: Food and exercise image analysis

These services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties, except:

  • With your assigned Dealer (who manages your account), who can view your health diary entries
  • With App administrators for account management purposes
  • When required by law

7. Your Rights

  • Access your personal data at any time through the App
  • Request deletion of your account and data by contacting us
  • Update your profile information within the App

8. Children's Privacy

Niva Health AI is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes through the App.
